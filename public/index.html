A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

What comes in a Mahjinnies pack?

A hand-curated collection of themed trinkets in a 2.5 × 2.5 inch clear display frame. The number of pieces depends on the collection; explore a pack to see its details.

Will my pack look exactly like the photo?

The displayed box is a sample. Your box will contain a random assortment of related items from the same collection, so the exact combination may differ from the photo.

How do we use them at game night?

Bring your Mahjinnies to your next Mahjong game night. Whenever someone wins a hand, give them one from your pack. Invite your friends to bring theirs, too, and turn each win into a little keepsake.

What makes each pack special?

Xibie chooses the pieces, pairs colors and styles, and arranges each frame by hand. Every pack is a mini collection built around a theme.

Are Mahjinnies toys?

No. They are decorative collectibles. WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD. Small parts. Not for children under 5 years.
